MediaFlow - Iteration 4 (final) - things done (as of 2026-11-16, branch iteration-4)

FEATURES, FIRST ROUND
1. Today is customisable. Each panel (the numbers row, Suggestions for
   today, Did these happen?, Planned today, Your day, Backlog, Coming up)
   has a Hide link, and a "Customize this page" box lists every panel with a
   checkbox. Hidden panels are remembered per user. The heading and Suggest
   something always show.
2. The calendar shows how busy a day is: commitments (amber) ahead of
   planned sittings (green) in each cell, three per cell and "+N more", with
   the day's busy time in the corner. Commitments over midnight show on both
   days.
3. Click a day to add to it: "+ Plan something" puts a backlog item on that
   date at a time you pick; "+ Commitment" opens the commitment form on that
   date and comes back to the calendar.
4. Long free gaps are split: a sitting, a 15-minute break, the next best
   thing, while at least 45 minutes remain.
5. Display name and anonymous reviews.
6. Log time by hand, on a date, counted on Progress.
7. Background push reminders, opt-in with VAPID keys and a sender process.
8. Catalog edits survive imports ("Keep my edits"); seed_catalog no longer
   crashes on a duplicated title.
9. PostgreSQL option through DATABASE_URL and a compose overlay.

FEATURES, SECOND ROUND
10. Move or remove a planned sitting. Planned cards have Move (the plan form
    prefilled, with a date field, its own slot not counted as busy) and
    Remove (frees the time, nothing held against the title).
11. "Did these happen?" on Today: planned sittings whose time has passed and
    were never marked. Yes logs the time; Didn't happen removes it.
12. Exceptions to recurring blocks: skip one block on one date from the
    calendar day panel, or mark the whole day off. Skipped blocks show
    struck through with restore, and My week lists coming exceptions.
13. Episode and chapter tracking: position derived from minutes and the
    unit length ("Episode 5 of 24 next"), an optional per-item unit length
    (a 60-minute drama), "episodes already finished" on the edit form, and
    suggestions that say which episodes they cover.
14. Weekly goal: minutes per week set on My week or Settings, shown as a bar
    on Today and a card on Progress.
15. Community ratings in the scoring: half a priority step, scaled by other
    students' average rating of the same catalog title.
16. Sittings across midnight on overnight schedules. The evening gap knows
    how far into the next date it may run; a film that only fits 22:30 to
    01:00 is offered, stored on the date it starts, and blocks the small
    hours of the next date once accepted.
17. Settings page gathering waking hours, weekly goal, display name, Today
    panels, reminders, subscribed browsers (with remove), password change,
    data export and account deletion. The nav links to it.
18. Install as an app: web manifest, generated icons (manage.py make_icons),
    theme colour and apple-touch-icon.
19. Export my data as one JSON file; delete my account after typing the
    password, with everything cascading.
20. Import a calendar (.ics) on My week: weekly repeats become recurring
    blocks (kind guessed from the title), one-off events in the next year
    become commitments, all-day and past events are left out with a count.
21. Import a Letterboxd or Goodreads export on Backlog: catalog matches
    preferred by year, the rest added by title, Goodreads shelves become
    statuses and ratings become private reviews.
22. Look up a title on demand from Discover: asks Open Library, TMDB and
    RAWG for the search term right now and adds what comes back.

FEATURES, THIRD ROUND (polish and the last ideas)
31. Mobile pass. Calendar cells show a busy dot instead of the label on
    phones, with a swipe hint; backlog action rows wrap; buttons never
    break mid-word; tap targets are at least 32px.
32. Accessibility pass. A skip-to-content link, a main landmark that
    receives focus, one visible focus ring for everything keyboard-reachable,
    aria-current on the active nav link and the selected calendar day,
    calendar cells described for screen readers ("Tuesday, October 6, 2h
    busy, 1 item"), named Remove, Move, skip and restore buttons, and a
    polite live region for flash messages.
33. Later. A third answer to a suggestion, next to Not today: the title is
    back in the running from tomorrow with nothing held against it.
34. Time-of-day preference. The scoring learns from what you accept and
    turn down per media type and part of the day (morning, afternoon,
    evening, night) over the last sixty days; half a priority step, once
    there are at least two answers to go on.
35. Semester view. Busy hours per week for the term from classes, shifts,
    exceptions and commitments, with the busiest and lightest weeks and the
    average, 4 to 26 weeks, linked from My week and the calendar.
36. Series continuation. Catalog rows carry a series and a position; films
    get it from TMDB collections on import. The catalog page shows what
    comes next, and Reviews offers the next title after you finish one.
37. Watch together. From a catalog page, name a classmate and the planner
    lists the next two weeks' stretches free for both; pick one and they
    get an invite on Today. Accepting puts an accepted sitting on both
    calendars (and the title on their backlog). Decline and withdraw too.
38. Read-only JSON API. Bearer tokens made and revoked on Settings, stored
    hashed and shown once. /api/v1/me/, backlog/, day/?date=, progress/.
